    The classic rhythm: 25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes off, and a 15-minute break after every fourth round. Francesco Cirillo built the method around a tomato-shaped kitchen timer in the late 1980s, which is where the name comes from (pomodoro is Italian for tomato).
